Privacy Policy

At ShopHub, your privacy is important to us. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have.

1. Information we collect

We collect information you provide directly to us, such as:

  • Account data: name, email, phone, password, delivery addresses
  • Transaction data: orders, payment methods, purchase history
  • Usage data: pages visited, products viewed, search queries
  • Device data: IP address, browser type, OS, device identifiers

2. How we use your information

We use the information to:

  • Process orders and provide customer support
  • Personalize your shopping experience
  • Send order updates and promotional communications (you can opt out)
  • Detect and prevent fraud or abuse
  • Improve our platform and services

3. Sharing and disclosure

We never sell your personal information. We may share limited data with:

  • Vendors: to fulfill your orders (name, address, phone only)
  • Payment processors: to process transactions securely
  • Delivery partners: to ship your items
  • Legal authorities: if required by law

4. Your rights

You have the right to:

  • Access, correct, or delete your personal data
  • Opt out of marketing communications
  • Request a copy of your data
  • Close your account at any time

5. Cookies

We use cookies and similar technologies to keep you logged in, remember your preferences, and analyze site traffic. You can control cookies through your browser settings.
