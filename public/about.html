How we started

ShopHub was founded in 2019 with a simple idea: make shopping easy and empower small businesses. What began as a team of 5 in a small Dhaka office has grown into a platform serving millions.

Today we connect thousands of vendors — from home-based entrepreneurs to established brands — with customers across the country. Our goal is to keep growing while staying true to our core values.
